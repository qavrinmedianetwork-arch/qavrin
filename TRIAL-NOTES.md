# QAVRIN TRIAL NOTES

This build is intentionally simple.

The product question being tested is:

> Will Indian users create an account and come back to write and read other people's thoughts?

The trial avoids:
- avatars
- follower counts
- complex notifications
- video
- advertising
- payment
- endless profile customisation

The goal is to validate writing + reading + discussion first.

Success metrics for the first test:
1. 20+ people create accounts
2. At least 30% of signups create a post
3. At least 20% return and post/read again within 7 days
4. Posts receive real comments, not only likes
5. Users can explain in one sentence why QAVRIN is different
